CYBER BIRD — original city map for standalone ASHFALL

Original architecture, procedural city layout, avian sculptures, feather signs,
district identities, weathered material shader and map integration authored for
this project by Codex, October 2026. No downloaded city, bird or texture packs.
The new city materials use analytic shader detail and shared primitive geometry.

Inherited ASHFALL code, weapons, audio, hands and other assets retain their
existing provenance and license files. The delivery's licenses/ directory is
copied without modification from the verified standalone9-9-2 checkpoint.
Gemini's inherited models are used as already integrated in that checkpoint;
neither the original models nor any external original asset folders were edited.
