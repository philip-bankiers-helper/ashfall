Run Launch-VR.cmd manually when ready. This candidate uses its own Feedback069 save/settings profile and does not change Library or Quest launch targets.

Duplicate gun models retain separate ammo and reload states.

Shake the shield shaker in any direction until its percentage becomes READY and its ready sound plays, then tilt to drink. No lid removal. Open soda with the other hand. Banana: grip near the top with the other hand and pull downward four times, releasing/regripping between strokes, then bring it to the mouth.

With the weapon stowed, B/Y cycles build shape. Hold trigger for preview, release to place. Unsupported placement is rejected. Settings has Locomotion, Set Height, Weapons and General; Set Height reinitializes VCS. Standing above a seated calibration cannot exceed the calibrated five-foot head top; crouching remains available.
